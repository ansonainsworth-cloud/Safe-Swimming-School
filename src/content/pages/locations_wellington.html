Swim lessons that come to you

Wellington families can take lessons at the pool they already use. We come to home pools and HOA community pools in the village, so there is no drive to a crowded public pool.

Safe Swimming School was founded by Cortney Berry, who has 20 years of swim-teaching experience, with cofounder Anson Ainsworth, who has 18 years of swim-teaching experience. Our instructors are certified, and we are fully insured.

Neighborhoods we serve in Wellington

We teach at home pools across the village, including Palm Beach Polo, Equestrian Club Estates, Olympia, Versailles, and Mizner Estates, and every neighborhood in between. Don't see yours? Text us; we likely reach your pool.

What to expect

  • One-on-one: focused time with a certified instructor. No group swim lessons.
  • Semi-private for siblings: brothers and sisters can learn together.
  • At your pool: home pools and HOA community pools across Wellington.
  • Every day, 8 AM–7 PM: book times that fit your week.
  • The same instructor every lesson.
  • Privacy on request: we're happy to sign an NDA for families who want privacy.

More about how we teach: Private swim lessons.

Ages 3 months to adult

  • Infants (from 3 months): start with a parent in the water, then move to private lessons.
  • Toddlers and kids: water comfort, breath control, floating, kicking and strokes, at your child's pace.
  • Adults and nervous swimmers: see Adult swim lessons in Palm Beach County.

FAQ

Do you come to my pool in Wellington?

Yes. We teach at home pools and community pools in Wellington. Tell us your pool type when you text.

What ages do you teach?

From 3 months to adults. Infants start with a parent in the water.

Do you offer group swim lessons?

No. Swim lessons are private, or semi-private for siblings learning together.

When are lessons available?

Every day, 8 AM to 7 PM.

Will we have the same instructor each lesson?

Yes. You keep the same instructor for every lesson.

Can you keep our family's information private?

Yes. We're happy to sign an NDA for families who want privacy.

How much do lessons cost?

8 lessons $520, 12 lessons $720, 20 lessons $1,100, plus a $35 annual registration. A single lesson is $75.
