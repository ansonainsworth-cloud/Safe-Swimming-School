Skills we practice

Our survival swim approach teaches safety skills first, then strokes. Swimmers practice rolling to their back, floating and resting, and swimming to the wall. A cue we use for floating is "you have to be flat to float." Swimmers start as young as 3 months, with a parent in the water, and we teach adults too.

Swim skills are one layer of protection. Nothing replaces adult supervision and pool barriers.
