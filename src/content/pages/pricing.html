Swim Lesson Pricing

Safe Swimming School offers private one-on-one swim lessons in Northern Palm Beach County, sold in packages of 8, 12, or 20 lessons.

Lesson Packages

  • 8 lessons: $520 ($65 per lesson)
  • 12 lessons: $720 ($60 per lesson)
  • 20 lessons: $1,100 ($55 per lesson)
  • Annual registration fee: $35 per year, listed separately
  • Single lesson: $75 (can be credited toward a package)

What’s Included

  • Private one-on-one instruction from our instructors, led by founder Cortney Berry and cofounder Anson (18 years swim teaching experience). Fully insured.
  • Lessons at your home or community pool in Palm Beach County
  • Available 8 AM to 7 PM, seven days a week
  • Lessons are designed around the swimmer’s age, comfort level, and goals

Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice count as a lesson taught.
  • Weather cancellations do not count and are added to the end of the package.

Areas we serve: Jupiter · Palm Beach Gardens · North Palm Beach · Tequesta · Jupiter Farms · Westlake · Juno Beach · Riviera Beach
