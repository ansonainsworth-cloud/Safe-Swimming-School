Swim Lesson Pricing

Safe Swimming School offers private one-on-one swim lessons across Palm Beach County, including West Palm Beach and surrounding areas, sold in packages of 8, 12, or 20 lessons.

Lesson Packages

  • 8 lessons: $520 ($65 per lesson)
  • 12 lessons: $720 ($60 per lesson)
  • 20 lessons: $1,100 ($55 per lesson)
  • Annual registration fee: $35 per year, listed separately
  • Single lesson: $75 (can be credited toward a package)
  • Water aerobics: $45 per 30-minute group class (details)

What’s Included

  • Private one-on-one instruction from our instructors, led by founder Cortney Berry, with 20 years of swim teaching experience, and cofounder Anson, with 18 years of swim teaching experience. Fully insured.
  • Lessons at your home or community pool in Palm Beach County
  • Available 8 AM to 7 PM, seven days a week
  • Lessons are designed around the swimmer’s age, comfort level, and goals

Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice count as a lesson taught.
  • Weather cancellations do not count and are added to the end of the package.

FAQ

How much do swim lessons cost?

Lessons are 30 minutes and private, at your home or community pool. Packages: 8 lessons for $520, 12 lessons for $720, or 20 lessons for $1,100.

Can we try a single lesson first?

Yes. A single lesson is $75, and we credit it toward a package if you continue.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes, it's an annual fee: $35 for one swimmer, $50 for two siblings, and $60 for a family with three or more swimmers.

Can siblings learn together?

Yes. Siblings can share a semi-private lesson at the same per-lesson price, and they can use one shared package. We don't offer group swim lessons.

Do you offer water aerobics?

Yes. Water aerobics is a group class, $45 for 30 minutes.

Where do lessons happen?

At your pool, anywhere in Palm Beach County, including West Palm Beach and surrounding areas. Lessons run 8 AM to 7 PM, every day.

What if it's too cold to swim?

We reschedule to the next available time that works for you, or move the lesson to the end of your package.
