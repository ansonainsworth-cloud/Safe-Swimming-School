Who our Palm Beach Gardens lessons are for

  • Infants and toddlers: We teach swimmers as young as 3 months. Infant lessons start with a parent in the water, then move to private lessons. Toddler lessons focus on water comfort, breath control, back floating, and early kicking and arm movements, at a pace your child can handle.
  • Kids: Help a school-age swimmer gain skills and confidence with focused one-on-one time.
  • Siblings: Brothers and sisters can learn together in semi-private lessons.
  • Adults: It's never too late. Private lessons for adults learning to swim or improving stroke technique. See adult swim lessons in Palm Beach County.
  • Nervous swimmers: One-on-one attention is especially helpful for swimmers who are nervous or fearful around water. We build comfort step by step.

Managing an HOA, 55+ or senior community pool? See our water aerobics classes for communities.

How it works

  1. Text 561-236-8260 with your swimmer's age, your Palm Beach Gardens neighborhood and pool access, your main goal (water safety, learning to swim, stroke improvement), and preferred days and times.
  2. We confirm availability and plan the first lesson around your swimmer.
  3. Choose a package and pay the annual registration ($35 for one swimmer, $50 for two siblings, and $60 for a family with three or more swimmers).
  4. We come to your pool. Lessons run 8 AM to 7 PM, every day of the week.

Packages

Same packages as the rest of our service area. There are no Palm Beach Gardens-only rates.

Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice count as a lesson taught. Weather cancellations don't count; those lessons are added to the end of your package. See Swim Lesson Pricing.
