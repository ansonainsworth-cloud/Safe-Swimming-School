Who our Palm Beach Gardens lessons are for

  • Infants and toddlers: We teach swimmers as young as 3 months. Infant lessons start with a parent in the water, then move to private lessons. Toddler lessons focus on water comfort, breath control, back floating, and early kicking and arm movements, at a pace your child can handle.
  • Kids: Help a school-age swimmer gain skills and confidence with focused one-on-one time.
  • Siblings: Brothers and sisters can learn together in semi-private lessons.
  • Adults: It's never too late. Private lessons for adults learning to swim or improving stroke technique. See adult swim lessons in Palm Beach County.
  • Nervous swimmers: One-on-one attention is especially helpful for swimmers who are nervous or fearful around water. We build comfort step by step.
