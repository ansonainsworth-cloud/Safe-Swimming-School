A gentle start for nervous swimmers

Some children (and adults) feel anxious around water, especially after a scary moment. We go at your swimmer's pace. The first lessons focus on comfort: getting wet, breathing, and trusting the instructor. Your own pool and the same instructor each time keep things familiar. We never rush a child who is not ready.

Skills we practice

Our survival swim approach teaches safety skills first, then strokes. Swimmers practice rolling to their back, floating and resting, and swimming to the wall. A cue we use for floating is "you have to be flat to float." As skills grow, we add kicking and strokes. Swimmers start as young as 3 months, with a parent in the water, and we teach adults too.

Swim skills are one layer of protection. Nothing replaces adult supervision and pool barriers.
