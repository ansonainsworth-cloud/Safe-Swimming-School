About Safe Swimming School

Safe Swimming School helps Palm Beach County families build safer, more confident swimmers through private one-on-one swim lessons at the client’s home or community pool.

Safe Swimming School was founded by Cortney Berry. Our cofounder, Anson, brings 18 years of swim teaching experience. We work with infants to adults, beginners, nervous swimmers, and students who need help with water safety or stroke technique.

Why Private Lessons?

Private swim lessons allow the instructor to focus entirely on one swimmer’s comfort level, goals, and progress. That one-on-one attention is especially helpful for beginners, young children, adults learning later in life, and swimmers who are nervous or fearful around water.

Current Service Area

Safe Swimming School currently serves Palm Beach County only. Preferred areas include Jupiter, Palm Beach Gardens, North Palm Beach, Tequesta, Jupiter Farms, Westlake, Juno Beach, and Riviera Beach. Travel throughout Palm Beach County may be available depending on schedule and location.
