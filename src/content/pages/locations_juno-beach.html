A1A, US-1, and Juno Ridge

Along A1A, families often swim in a condo or community pool at an oceanfront building. West of US-1, a home pool is more common. Nearby Juno Ridge, the census-designated place just inland of the town, is another example, not a limit. If your street is not named here, text us and ask. ZIP 33408 is the town ZIP, and North Palm Beach uses that ZIP too. See swim lessons in North Palm Beach if you are sorting out which page fits.

Juno Beach Pier and Loggerhead Marinelife Center in Loggerhead Park, at 14200 U.S. Highway 1, are landmarks that locate the town. The ocean, the pier, the park, and the Center are the setting. Lessons are not held at those places. They are at the pool your family can use. If that pool is in a building or a community, check the rules before the first lesson. We can come only when the family has access and the building allows an instructor to visit.

Swimmers practice how to roll to back, float and rest, and swim to the wall, then work on strokes. These skills are one layer of protection. Nothing replaces adult supervision and pool barriers. Being close to the Atlantic does not change that.

Finding the right pool in a small town

A useful way to picture Juno Beach is a short drive, not a big city grid. From the beach road to houses west of US-1 can take only a few minutes, yet the pools are different. On the ocean side, the pool is often shared by a building. Inland, it is often a backyard the family owns. Juno Ridge sits just past that inland edge. None of those settings is the whole town.

People giving directions sometimes use the pier or Loggerhead Park. The Center's published address, 14200 U.S. Highway 1, is a map pin, not a lesson address. If someone says the pool is near the pier, ask which building or which house. The instructor needs the pool the family can actually enter.

ZIP 33408 covers this town and also reaches into North Palm Beach. A pool with that ZIP might sit on either side of the town line. Send the street when you text, and we will say whether this page or the North Palm Beach page is the closer description. The lesson is still private, and it is still at your pool.

A breeze off the Atlantic does not move the lesson onto the sand. If a building asks for a name at the gate, send that request with your first text so the instructor arrives with the right details. Home pools west of US-1 usually skip that step. Tell us which kind of pool you have so the first lesson starts on time.

Siblings who live in the same house can share one package and take a semi-private lesson together. Two households in the same building do not share a lesson. Each booking stays with one household.
