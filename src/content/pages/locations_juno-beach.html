Private Swim Lessons in Juno Beach

Private, one-on-one swim lessons for Juno Beach families, at your home pool or community pool. Call or text 561-236-8260 to schedule · Enroll online

Lessons start with safety skills, then strokes. Swimmers practice how to roll to back, float and rest, and swim to the wall. These skills are one layer of protection. Nothing replaces adult supervision and pool barriers.

Swim lessons that come to you

Juno Beach is a small oceanfront town between Jupiter and North Palm Beach, and many families here live near the water, from oceanfront condos along A1A to homes west of US-1. We teach at your home pool or your building or community pool, so lessons happen in water your swimmer already knows.

Safe Swimming School was founded by Cortney Berry, who has 20 years of swim-teaching experience, with cofounder Anson Ainsworth, who has 18 years of swim-teaching experience. Our instructors are certified, and we are fully insured.

What to expect

  • One-on-one: focused time with a certified instructor. No group lessons.
  • Semi-private for siblings: brothers and sisters can learn together.
  • At your pool: condo and community pools along A1A, and home pools across Juno Beach and nearby Juno Ridge.
  • Every day, 8 AM–7 PM: book times that fit your week.
  • Privacy on request: we're happy to sign an NDA for families who want privacy.

More about how we teach: Private swim lessons.

Ages 3 months to adult

  • Infants (from 3 months): start with a parent in the water, then move to private lessons.
  • Toddlers and kids: water comfort, breath control, floating, kicking and strokes, at your child's pace.
  • Adults and nervous swimmers: see Adult swim lessons in Palm Beach County.
