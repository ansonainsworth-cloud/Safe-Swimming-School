Safe Swimming School helps Northern Palm Beach County families build safer, more confident swimmers through private one-on-one lessons. We come to your home or community pool. Our instructors are led by founder Cortney Berry, with 20 years of swim teaching experience, and cofounder Anson, with 18 years of swim teaching experience.

Now accepting new students in Jupiter, Palm Beach Gardens, North Palm Beach, Tequesta, Jupiter Farms, Westlake, Juno Beach, Riviera Beach, and surrounding areas.

Private Lessons That Fit Your Swimmer

Every swimmer is different. Lessons can focus on water safety, beginner skills, confidence around water, floating, breathing, stroke development, or overcoming fear of the water.

  • Private one-on-one lessons
  • Same instructor every lesson
  • Lessons at your home or community pool
  • Ages 3 months to adults, including nervous beginners
  • Available 8 AM to 7 PM, seven days a week
  • Preferred North County areas: Jupiter, Palm Beach Gardens, North Palm Beach, Tequesta, Jupiter Farms, Westlake, Juno Beach, and Riviera Beach

Lesson Packages

Private lessons are sold in packages of 8, 12, or 20 lessons. There is a $35 annual registration fee.

  • 8 lessons: $520 ($65/lesson)
  • 12 lessons: $720 ($60/lesson)
  • 20 lessons: $1,100 ($55/lesson)
  • Annual registration fee: $35
  • Single lesson: $75 (can be credited toward a package)

Not sure which package is right? Text the swimmer’s age, location, pool access, and main goal to 561-236-8260.

How to Get Started

Text 561-236-8260 with:

  1. Swimmer’s age
  2. City or neighborhood
  3. Whether you have a home or community pool
  4. Main goal: water safety, beginner swimming, fear of water, stroke improvement, or something else
  5. Best days and times
