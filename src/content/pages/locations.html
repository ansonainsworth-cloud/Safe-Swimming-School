FAQ

Do you come to my pool?

Yes. We teach at home pools and community pools across Palm Beach County, including West Palm Beach and surrounding areas.

What ages do you teach?

Ages 3 months to adults — private lessons paced to the swimmer. Text us with your swimmer’s age and goals.

How do I get started?

Text 561-236-8260, or complete the form on our Enroll page. Annual registration is $35 for one swimmer, $50 for two siblings, and $60 for a family with three or more swimmers, plus your package. A single lesson is $75 and can be credited toward a package.

Where can I see pricing?

Packages are 8 lessons for $520, 12 for $720, and 20 for $1,100, plus annual registration of $35 for one swimmer, $50 for two siblings, and $60 for a family with three or more swimmers. See Pricing.
