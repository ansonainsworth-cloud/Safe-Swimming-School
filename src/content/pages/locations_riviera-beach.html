Lessons start with safety skills, then strokes. Swimmers practice how to roll to back, float and rest, and swim to the wall. These skills are one layer of protection. Nothing replaces adult supervision and pool barriers.

Lessons are led by founder Cortney Berry, with 20 years of experience, and cofounder Anson Ainsworth, with 18 years of experience. Instructors are certified, and Safe Swimming School is fully insured. You have the same instructor every lesson.
