Enroll in Private Swim Lessons

Private, one-on-one swim lessons at your home or community pool across Palm Beach County, including Jupiter, Palm Beach Gardens, North Palm Beach, Tequesta, Jupiter Farms, Westlake, Juno Beach, Riviera Beach, West Palm Beach, and surrounding areas, for ages 3 months to adults. Lessons are taught by our instructors, led by founder Cortney Berry, with 20 years of experience, and cofounder Anson, with 18 years of experience. Safe Swimming School is fully insured.

How to enroll

  1. Text 561-236-8260 (or email info@safeswimmingschool.com) with your swimmer’s age, your town or neighborhood, whether you have a home or community pool, the main goal, and your best days and times.
  2. Choose a package and complete the registration form below.
  3. Start lessons. Lessons run 8 AM to 7 PM, seven days a week.
