AT-HOME, PRIVATE SWIM LESSONS IN NORTHERN PALM BEACH COUNTY

Private, one-on-one swim lessons at your home or community pool in Northern Palm Beach County, for ages 3 months to adults.

We believe convenience matters. We come directly to your home or community pool in Northern Palm Beach County. Our instructors are led by founder Cortney Berry and cofounder Anson, who has 18 years of experience. Learning in your own pool also means your swimmer develops skills right where they’re most likely to be near water — building real-world safety, not just pool confidence.

Every lesson is fully personalized. We customize each session to your swimmer’s age, skill level, goals, and comfort. No two swimmers are alike, so no two lessons are the same.

We teach all ages and all skill levels — infants (from 3 months), toddlers, children, teens, and adults.

SWIM LESSON PACKAGES
