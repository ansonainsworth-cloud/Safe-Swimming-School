Home swim lessons at your pool in Palm Beach County

Safe Swimming School offers home swim lessons and in-home swim lessons at your home pool or community pool across Palm Beach County, including West Palm Beach and surrounding areas. We come to you, so practice happens in the water your swimmer already knows. These are survival swim lessons taught one-on-one, starting with safety skills, then strokes.

What a lesson at your pool looks like

Your instructor arrives at the pool your family already uses. Lessons are private and one-on-one, and each one runs 30 minutes. We schedule lessons any day of the week, from 8 AM to 7 PM, including after school and evenings.

Lessons start with safety skills, then strokes. Swimmers practice how to roll to back, float and rest, and swim to the wall. These skills are one layer of protection. Nothing replaces adult supervision and pool barriers. Each session is designed around the swimmer's age, comfort level, and goals.

We teach ages 3 months to adults. Infants begin with a parent in the water, then move to private lessons. Toddlers work on water comfort, breath control, back floating, and early kicking and arm movements, at a pace they can handle. School-age kids, teens, and adults are welcome too.

Same instructor every lesson

You have the same instructor every lesson. Our instructors are certified and fully insured. They are led by founder Cortney Berry, with 20 years of swim teaching experience, and cofounder Anson Ainsworth, with 18 years of swim teaching experience. If your family wants privacy, we are happy to sign an NDA.

What your family needs

The main thing you need is pool access. That can be a home pool or a community pool, including an HOA pool your family can use. Tell us your pool type and preferred days when you text, and we will confirm availability.

To get started, text or call 561-236-8260 with the swimmer's age, your city or neighborhood, whether you have a home or community pool, your main goal, and your best days and times. You can also book online through our Enroll page, or email info@safeswimmingschool.com.

Nervous swimmers, at their own pace

Nervous swimmers are welcome. Lessons move at a pace that fits the swimmer, with time to build water comfort and skills step by step. Instructors can adjust to how your swimmer is doing that day. There is no group-class pressure, because we do not offer group swim lessons. Lessons stay private and one-on-one. Siblings can share a semi-private lesson at the same per-lesson price, and they can use one shared package.

Packages and registration

Private lessons are sold in packages of 8, 12, or 20:

  • 8 lessons for $520 ($65 per lesson)
  • 12 lessons for $720 ($60 per lesson)
  • 20 lessons for $1,100 ($55 per lesson)

Annual registration is $35 for one swimmer, $50 for two siblings, and $60 for a family with three or more swimmers. A single lesson is $75 and can be credited toward a package if you continue.

If you buy a package by December 31, 2026, annual registration is waived and those lessons never expire. Full details are on our pricing page. If you are buying for someone else, packages can also be given as a gift.

Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice count as a lesson taught. If the weather is too cold to swim, we reschedule to the next available time that works for you, or move the lesson to the end of your package.
