Water Safety and Drowning Prevention in Palm Beach County

  • May 1, 2026
  • safeswimming
  • Water Safety

Florida loses more children under 5 to drowning than any other state — and Palm Beach County families face this reality every single day. Water safety education is not just a nice-to-have — it’s a necessity.

The Numbers Are Stark

  • Drowning is the leading cause of death for children ages 1–4 in the United States
  • In Florida, young children drown at a higher rate than in any other state, and Florida’s overall drowning rate is well above the national average
  • For children ages 1–4, most drownings happen in home swimming pools — often when a child slips away during a non-swim time. It can happen in seconds.
  • Drowning is fast and often silent. There’s usually no splashing or screaming.

What Every Palm Beach County Parent Needs to Know

An NIH study found formal swim lessons were linked to an 88% lower drowning risk for children ages 1–4. A child who can do a few strokes is not water safe. True water safety means knowing how to roll to their back and float until help arrives, get to the wall from any direction, and stay calm in unexpected submersion.

How Safe Swimming School Approaches Water Safety

Every lesson includes water safety fundamentals alongside stroke development. Our instructors teach water safety fundamentals from the very first lesson — even with infants as young as 3 months. Infant lessons start with a parent in the water, then move to private lessons. We teach: back floating, breath control, getting to the wall from any position, and building comfort in the water.

Private Lessons = Faster Safety

In a private one-on-one lesson, every minute is focused on your child’s specific needs.
