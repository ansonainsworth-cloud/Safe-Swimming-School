Water Safety and Drowning Prevention in Palm Beach County

  • May 1, 2026
  • safeswimming
  • Water Safety

Palm Beach County families spend a lot of time around pools. Water skills belong in that routine, alongside adult supervision and pool barriers.

What the lessons cover

Survival swim lessons start with safety skills, then strokes. Swimmers learn to roll to back, float and rest, and swim to the wall. A few strokes on their own are not the whole lesson. These skills are one layer of protection. Nothing replaces adult supervision and pool barriers.

How lessons work

Our instructors teach these water skills from the first lesson, including with infants as young as 3 months. Infant lessons start with a parent in the water, then move to private lessons. Lessons also include breath control and comfort in the water.

Private lessons

In a private one-on-one lesson, every minute is focused on your child’s specific needs.

Serving Palm Beach County

Safe Swimming School provides private swim lessons throughout Palm Beach County, including West Palm Beach and surrounding areas, 7 days a week at your home or community pool.
