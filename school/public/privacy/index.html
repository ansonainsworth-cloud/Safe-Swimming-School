Safe Swimming School

Privacy

We teach private swim lessons at your family pool in Palm Beach County. This page says what this app stores.

Who can sign in

Parents, instructors, and the admin sign in with an email link. There is no password. The link expires in 30 minutes.

Children

We store a child by first name only. We do not ask for a last name, a photo, or a birth date in this app.

Your lessons

Parents see their own upcoming lessons and how many lessons the family has left. Brothers and sisters share one number. Parents see their own instructor. They do not see other families.

Location

The instructor phone can share location only while they are on the way to your pool or during the lesson. Sharing stops at clock out. Clock in checks that the instructor is at the pool. We keep the distance result for that check. We do not keep a long trail of map points.

When live location is turned on, raw location points are deleted within 24 hours.

Texts

The school can text the family when the instructor is about 10 minutes away. That text goes to the parent for that lesson. You will not get a location text for another family. This text is saved as a setting now and will be sent in a later update.

Lesson records

Lesson times come from the school calendar named Anson Ainsworth Swim. Lesson counts come from the school lesson tracker. When a lesson is clocked out, the family count goes down by one. An admin can undo that.

What we do not do

We do not sell this information. We do not use it for advertising.

Questions

Ask Anson if something here is unclear.
